Gate Types

Swing Gates and Sliding Gates

Space usually decides which one fits, and either type can secure a driveway.

2 Key PointsWhat to expect
01

Swing Gates

Swing gates pivot open on hinges, as a single leaf or a double pair. They suit properties with room for the gate to sweep clear of the driveway, and they offer a classic, elegant look.

02

Sliding Gates

A sliding gate travels sideways along the fence line, which suits properties with limited room in front of the opening. The motion is smooth and the look is sleek and modern. It does need clear space alongside the opening for the gate to slide.

Access Control

Ways to Open an Automatic Gate

Pick the access method that matches who uses the gate every day.

01

Keypad Entry

Enter a code to open the gate. You can manage separate codes for family, staff, and deliveries.

02

Remote Control

A handheld remote opens the gate from your vehicle. It is the simplest option for daily use.

03

Smartphone App

Control the gate from your phone and, where the system supports it, get notifications of gate activity.

Safety and Backup

Safety Features and Backup Power

A gate is heavy moving equipment. Plan for safety and for power outages before you buy.

5 Things to Ask AboutBefore you choose

Obstruction Sensors

Sensors and edge devices detect a person, pet, or vehicle in the path and stop or reverse the gate.

Manual Release

A release lets you open the gate by hand if the operator loses power or fails.

Backup Power

Battery backup can keep the gate operating through a power outage.

Safety Standards

Look for a gate operator that meets the UL 325 safety standard for gate operators.

Professional Installation

Automatic gates involve moving weight, electrical work, and safety devices. A professional installer is the safer route.

Good to Know

Baton Rouge Automatic Gate Questions, Answered

What Baton Rouge property owners ask before adding a gate.

6 Common QuestionsStraight answers
Should I Choose a Swing Gate or a Sliding Gate?
Swing gates need clear room to open across the driveway, and sliding gates need room alongside the opening. Measure both spaces and talk it through with an installer before you decide.
Can an Automatic Gate Work for My Baton Rouge Business?
Yes. Automatic gates are increasingly common on commercial properties, where access control and security matter. Keypads, remotes, and app control are the usual options.
What Happens to the Gate When the Power Goes Out?
Most systems have a manual release, and battery backup can keep the gate running. With storms a possibility in Baton Rouge, ask about both.
Are Automatic Gates Safe Around Kids and Pets?
They can be, with the right safety devices, such as obstruction sensors and an operator that meets the UL 325 standard. Installation quality matters, so use a professional.
Can I Install an Automatic Gate Myself?
We recommend against it. Gate operators involve electrical work, moving weight, and safety devices, and mistakes can hurt someone. A professional installer is the safer route.
Which Access Method Fits a Baton Rouge Home?
Keypads suit households that share codes, remotes suit daily driving, and apps add remote control and notifications. You can combine more than one on the same gate.
