Care and Upkeep

Keeping an Iron Fence in Good Shape

Iron holds up for many years when the finish gets attention.

4 Care HabitsSimple and regular

Clean Regularly

Wash off dirt, sap, and debris. Built-up grime holds moisture against the metal.

Inspect Often

Look for chipped paint, small cracks, and orange or brown spots, especially near the ground and around joints.

Treat Rust Early

Surface rust can be brushed back and the spot repainted. Larger areas are worth a professional look.

Recoat When Needed

Fresh paint on a regular schedule keeps moisture out of the metal.

Good to Know

Baton Rouge Wrought Iron Fence Questions, Answered

What Baton Rouge property owners ask before choosing iron.

6 Common QuestionsStraight answers
Does a Wrought Iron Fence Rust in Baton Rouge?
Iron can rust when its protective finish wears through, and humid weather speeds that up. Regular cleaning, inspection, and prompt touch-ups keep the finish intact.
How Much Does a Wrought Iron Fence Cost in Baton Rouge?
Iron usually costs more up front than materials like wood or chain link. The final price depends on the design, height, amount of fence, finials, and gates. A free consultation gives you the real number for your property.
Can You Match the Style of My House?
Yes. Iron fences are designed around the architecture, from ornate and traditional to minimal and modern.
Do You Build Iron Fences for Businesses in Baton Rouge?
Yes. The same fence works for commercial properties, from retail storefronts to industrial facilities, with a design that suits the business.
How Long Does a Wrought Iron Fence Last?
Many years when it is installed properly and maintained. Cleaning, inspection, and early rust treatment make the difference.
Can I Add a Gate to My Iron Fence?
Yes. Gates can be made to match the fence pattern, and a gate can be built to work with an automatic opener when you want one.
