6 Timeline FactorsWhat changes the schedule

Size of the Area

The smaller the area you want to fence, the shorter the project.

Good to Know

Walker Metal Fence Questions, Answered

What Walker property owners ask about metal fencing.

6 Common QuestionsStraight answers
How Long Does It Take to Install a Metal Fence in Walker?
A basic metal fence takes about 2 to 4 days with professional installers. A custom-designed fence with specific details can take longer, and so can a large yard, rough soil, sloped ground, or several gates.
How Long Does a Metal Fence Last?
A protected hollow steel fence should last about 20 years. Solid steel, cast iron, and tubular aluminum fences can last even longer. Protecting the finish is what gets a fence the most life.
How Much Does a Metal Fence Cost in Walker?
For a 4-foot-tall fence, our Walker page lists about $8 to $12 per linear foot for aluminum, $7 to $10 for steel, and $25 to $30 for wrought iron. The metal, the amount of fence, labor, and accessories all affect the total, so a free estimate gives you the real number.
What Slows Down a Metal Fence Installation?
Yard size, weather, design complexity, and ground conditions all matter. Boulders, thick roots, and clay slow digging, sloped land adds effort, and each gate adds time. We factor these in when we give you a Walker estimate.
Which Metal Fence Works Best for a Pool?
Aluminum. It is rust-proof, strong, durable, and low maintenance, and we highly recommend it if you have a swimming pool.
Do You Offer Metal Fence Maintenance in Walker?
Yes. Our team can provide maintenance for metal fences. For wrought iron, dealing with rust or damage promptly, such as with rust-inhibiting paint each year, keeps the fence lasting.
