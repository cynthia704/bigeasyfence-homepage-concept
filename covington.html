About Covington

Covington is the seat of St. Tammany Parish, at a fork of the Bogue Falaya and Tchefuncte River. It is part of the Slidell-Mandeville-Covington statistical area and had 11,564 residents at the 2020 census.

A Short History of Covington

Europeans first settled here around 1800, and the town was established in 1813 by John Wharton Collins. It was first called Wharton, then renamed in 1816 for General Leonard Covington. Waterways and railroads fueled its growth, and in the 20th century many people moved here from New Orleans for affordable housing and a small-town feel.

Things to Do in Covington
  • Tammany Trace: A 31-mile bike trail that runs through several communities.
  • A Famous Statue: Covington is home to a statue billed as the World's Largest Ronald Reagan Statue.
Fun Facts About Covington
