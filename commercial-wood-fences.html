Good to Know

Commercial Wood Fence Questions, Answered

What business owners ask before installing a wood fence.

6 Common QuestionsStraight answers
Why Choose a Wood Fence for a Commercial Property?
A wood fence is a strong, durable barrier that deters unauthorized access, and it adds warmth and visual appeal to your property. It can be customized for privacy or to mark boundaries, and a well-designed fence reinforces your brand image.
Can the Fence Match My Building and Brand?
Yes. Our design experts create a wood fence design that fits your property's architecture and conveys your brand's image. We start with a consultation about your security needs, layout, and design preferences.
Will a Wood Fence Give Enough Privacy for Sensitive Areas?
It can. Whether you need to protect sensitive information or create a secure perimeter, wood fences offer the privacy and security a commercial space requires. Height and design are chosen around your needs.
Do You Offer Maintenance for Commercial Wood Fences?
Yes. We offer maintenance services to keep your wood fence looking pristine. For general upkeep tips, see Tips to extend the life of your wood fence in New Orleans.
How Long Will a Wood Fence Last in New Orleans?
It depends on the wood, the installation, and the upkeep. Our guide puts the average life of a wood fence in New Orleans at about 15 years, and up to 20 years or more with quality materials, proper installation, and regular maintenance.
What Does the Process Look Like From First Call to Finished Fence?
It starts with a free estimate and a consultation about your security needs, property layout, and design preferences. We then recommend suitable wood fence options and our team installs the fence aligned with your property's layout.
