Why Vinyl

Why Homeowners Pick Vinyl

The appeal comes down to the chores you get to skip.

5 Key BenefitsWhat vinyl delivers

No Painting or Staining

Vinyl is finished from the factory, so there is no repeating paint or stain cycle like wood has.

Pest and Rot Resistance

Termites cannot eat it, and it does not rot the way wood can.

Easy Cleaning

A hose and a soft brush usually handle dirt. A mild soap solution takes care of mildew, which is common in humid weather.

Wide Style Range

Picket, privacy, and ranch rail designs give you real choice.

Color Choices

White is the classic, and earthy tones are also available.

Upkeep

Caring for a Vinyl Fence

Vinyl asks for little, and these four habits cover it.

01

Rinse It

Hose the fence down when it looks dirty.

02

Scrub Spots

Use a soft brush and mild soap on mildew, algae, or stains, especially in shaded spots. Skip harsh abrasives that could scratch the surface.

03

Keep Plants Back

Trim vines and shrubs so moisture and growth do not sit against the panels.

04

Watch for Impact

A hard hit from a mower, trimmer, or vehicle can crack a panel. Have damaged sections repaired or replaced.

Before You Choose

Things to Weigh Before Choosing Vinyl

Vinyl is not right for every yard. Here is where to look first.

01

Privacy or Openness

Solid panels block the view, while picket and rail styles keep it open.

02

Neighborhood Rules

Some neighborhoods and associations set limits on fence height, style, and color. Check them before you order.

03

The Look You Want

Vinyl reads as clean and uniform. If you want the grain of natural wood, compare the two side by side.

04

Gates and Hardware

Plan gates in the same style from the start, since they take the most daily use.

Good to Know

Baton Rouge Vinyl Fence Questions, Answered

What Baton Rouge homeowners ask about vinyl.

6 Common QuestionsStraight answers
Is Vinyl a Good Fence for Baton Rouge Weather?
Vinyl does not rot and termites cannot eat it, which matters in humid south Louisiana. Storms and impacts can still damage any fence, so a professional installation and a look over the fence after bad weather are wise.
Does a Vinyl Fence Need Painting or Staining?
No. Unlike wood, it does not need periodic painting or staining. Rinsing it down is often all it needs.
What Vinyl Fence Styles Can I Get in Baton Rouge?
Picket, privacy, and ranch rail. Picket suits a front yard, privacy suits a backyard retreat, and ranch rail marks a boundary with a farmhouse look.
What Colors Does Vinyl Come In?
White is the classic choice, and earthy tones are available too. Ask about the current options during your design consultation.
How Do I Clean a Vinyl Fence?
Hose it down, and use a soft brush with mild soap on stubborn spots. Avoid abrasive pads that can scratch the surface.
Will a Vinyl Fence Give My Baton Rouge Yard Privacy?
Solid privacy panels screen the yard from view and can take some of the noise out of the surroundings. Picket and rail styles are open, so choose by how private you want the space.
