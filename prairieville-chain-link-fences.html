What It Is

What Is a Chain Link Fence

It is one of the oldest and most familiar fence types.

2 Key PointsWhat to expect
01

Woven Steel in a Diamond Pattern

A chain link fence is made of galvanized steel wire bent into a zigzag that forms a diamond mesh. You may hear it called a cyclone fence, hurricane fence, diamond-mesh fence, wire-mesh fence, or chain-wire fence. They all describe the same product.

02

Jobs It Does Well

Pet containment, backyard dog runs, and separate runs for large and small animals. Playgrounds, tennis courts, and baseball parks. Most of our clients choose chain link for safety and containment at a price that stays low.

How It Works

How Prairieville Chain Link Installation Works

A chain link fence goes up in clear stages, and the job moves quickly. Here is the order.

6 Install StagesFrom layout to gates

Establish Fence Lines

The route of the fence is laid out first.

Set the Posts

Posts go in with concrete so the fence has a firm foundation.

Add Fittings and Rails

Post fittings go on, followed by the top and bottom rail.

Run the Tension Wire

Tension wire helps hold the bottom of the mesh in line.

Stretch and Tie Off

The chain link fabric is stretched tight and tied off to the posts and rails.

Hang the Gates

Gates go in last, so the openings fit the finished fence.

Good to Know

Prairieville Chain Link Fence Questions, Answered

What Prairieville property owners ask about chain link.

6 Common QuestionsStraight answers
What Else Is Chain Link Fence Called?
You may see it called a cyclone fence, hurricane fence, diamond-mesh fence, wire-mesh fence, chain-wire fence, or wire netting. All of them are woven galvanized steel wire forming a diamond pattern.
How Much Does Chain Link Cost in Prairieville?
Our Prairieville page lists $3 to $6 per linear foot for a 4-foot residential fence and $8 to $15 per foot for a 6-foot fence, using 2-inch mesh and 9 or 11 gauge wire. A commercial-grade court fence of 8 to 12 feet runs $15 to $40 or more per foot.
Can Chain Link Be Used for Dog Runs and Pet Areas?
Yes. Pet containment at home, backyard dog runs, and separate areas for large and small animals are all common uses. Tell us the size of the animals and the space when you ask for a quote.
Does Chain Link Installation Take Long?
It goes up quickly. The job follows a set order: fence lines, posts, fittings, rails, tension wire, fabric, and gates. A Prairieville install does not stretch into months.
Can I Choose the Height and Color of My Fence?
Yes. You can pick the color and height to match your property and the purpose of the fence. For extra security, a heavier gauge with a larger wire diameter makes it much stronger.
Does Big Easy Fence Handle Maintenance in Prairieville?
Yes. Our chain link services cover maintenance and repairs as well as installation. Galvanized chain link cleans up easily, which keeps upkeep simple.
