Before We Start

What We Check Before We Build

A fence that looks right but lands in the wrong spot is a headache later, not a win now — this is standard on every commercial job we take on.

4 Pre-Build ChecksEvery job, every time

Property Lines & Easements

We confirm surveyed boundaries and shared easements before work starts — common on commercial lots split between tenants.

Zoning & Commercial Permits

Commercial fencing often needs its own permit and zoning sign-off, separate from a standard residential job.

Fire Lane & Access Compliance

We plan gates and fence lines around required fire-lane clearances and emergency access points.

Utility Marking

Louisiana One Call (811) marks underground lines before any post hole gets dug.
