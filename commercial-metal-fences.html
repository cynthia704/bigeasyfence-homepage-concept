Types of Metal Fences

Five Metal Fence Types for a Commercial Property

Each type suits a different job, budget, and level of security.

01

Chain Link Fence

Fast to install for securing a perimeter, and useful for separating storage areas for valuable goods inside a warehouse. Popular in industrial, construction, and warehouse businesses.

02

Barbed Wire or Razor Wire

Practical dividers for customers who need restrictions and a deterrent to unwelcome intruders.

03

Aluminum Fence

A customer favorite because it is rust-proof, which makes it a top recommendation around swimming pools. Available in picket style for view and airflow, or solid panel style for full privacy.

04

Welded Wire Mesh Fence

A cost-effective way to contain a large area, in the height you need. A first choice on many construction, civil, and utility projects.

05

Ornamental Steel Fence

Elegant, high-quality fencing that improves the exterior appearance of your business while adding value and security.

Good to Know

Commercial Metal Fence Questions, Answered

What business owners ask about metal fencing.

7 Common QuestionsStraight answers
What Types of Metal Fences Do You Install for Businesses?
Chain link, barbed or razor wire, aluminum, welded wire mesh, and ornamental steel. Each suits a different property type and level of security, and we help you choose.
Which Metal Fence Works Best Around a Swimming Pool?
Aluminum. It is rust-proof, so exposure to water is not a worry, which is why it is our top recommendation for properties with pools. It is also strong, durable, and low maintenance.
How Are Commercial Fence Panels Different From Residential Ones?
Commercial panels use larger pickets and rails and a thicker gauge of material, and each rail has eight metal support ribs. A standard commercial panel has pickets at 3-5/8 inch intervals, and some use 1-1/2 inch spacing for extra security.
How Much Does a Commercial Metal Fence Cost?
It depends on the material, yard shape, and season. A 4-foot prefab panel runs about $10 to $14 per linear foot uninstalled in tubular aluminum, starts at $15 in hollow steel, $84 in solid steel, and $125 in cast iron. Installed, a 100-foot fence runs about $1,000 to $3,500 in aluminum and $3,000 to $3,500 in steel.
Can I Get Privacy With a Metal Fence?
Yes. Aluminum can be installed in picket style for view and airflow, or in solid panel style for full privacy.
Can You Add Extra Security Features?
Yes. We can design and attach spears or other finials, use tighter picket spacing, or add barbed or razor wire where you need practical dividers and a deterrent to intruders.
What Works Best for a Large Area Like a Construction or Utility Site?
Welded wire mesh. It is a cost-effective way to contain a large area, in the height you need, and it is a first choice on many construction, civil, and utility projects.
