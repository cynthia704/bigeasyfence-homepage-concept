Why Aluminum

Why Aluminum Fences Suit New Orleans Homes

Aluminum earns its place in a lot of front and back yards. These are the reasons homeowners pick it.

6 Key BenefitsWhat aluminum delivers

Easy Maintenance

Aluminum resists rust and corrosion, which matters in New Orleans humidity. A regular hosing to remove dust and dirt is usually all it needs.

Budget-Friendly

It costs less than wrought iron and needs far less upkeep than wood, yet it still gives you a polished metal look.

Style and Color Choices

Picket, horizontal, and flat-top designs come in a wide range of colors. A powder-coated finish helps the color hold up to sun and rain.

Custom Sizes

Standard designs cover most yards, and custom posts, balusters, and heights are available. Aluminum is lightweight, which makes tailoring easier.

Handles Uneven Ground

Aluminum adapts well to sloped and uneven yards, where some other materials are harder to fit.

Pest and Weather Resistance

Aluminum does not rot, and termites have nothing to eat. It holds up to heavy rain and long, humid summers.

Common Uses

What Aluminum Fencing Does Well

One material, several jobs around a property.

01

Privacy

Closely spaced pickets or slats block more of the view, so you get a private yard while keeping a metal fence's clean lines.

02

Pool Enclosures

Open pickets keep a clear line of sight to the water while marking the pool area off from the rest of the yard.

03

Property Boundaries

A low or mid-height aluminum fence shows where your lot ends without closing off the view.

04

Decorative Accents

Arched tops, pointed finials, and rich colors add curb appeal along a front walk or driveway.

Durability

Built for Humid, Stormy Weather

New Orleans is tough on fences. Aluminum handles it well.

2 Key PointsWhat to expect
01

Rust-Free and Rot-Free

Because aluminum does not rust or rot, humid summers and heavy rain do little damage. Powder coating adds a tough finish that helps the color last. A rinse with the hose clears off dust and dirt.

02

Fits Slopes and Custom Heights

Uneven lots are no problem, since aluminum sections can follow the ground. Posts and balusters can be customized and heights are easy to vary, so one material can cover a front fence, a pool fence, and a side gate.

Styles

Popular Aluminum Fence Styles

Four designs come up again and again.

01

Two-Rail Flat Top

A clean, simple profile with two horizontal rails and a level top. A good fit for a modern look at a lower height.

02

Three-Rail Flat Top

A third rail adds a sturdier, more substantial look while keeping the same level top line.

03

Three-Rail With Exposed Pickets

The pickets rise above the top rail, which gives the fence a more traditional, ornamental look.

04

Aluminum Arches

A curved top adds softness and a decorative touch, and it is a popular choice along front walks and at gates.

Good to Know

Aluminum Fence Questions, Answered

What New Orleans homeowners ask before choosing aluminum.

6 Common QuestionsStraight answers
What Is an Aluminum Fence Used For?
Privacy, pool enclosures, property boundaries, and decorative accents. The design decides the job: closely spaced pickets for privacy, open pickets for pools and boundaries.
Does an Aluminum Fence Rust?
Aluminum resists rust and corrosion, which is a big reason it works well in New Orleans humidity. A powder-coated finish adds to that protection. Rinsing off dust and dirt with a hose is usually all the upkeep it needs.
How Does Aluminum Compare With Wrought Iron?
Aluminum gives a similar ornamental look for less money, and it needs less upkeep. Wrought iron is the more traditional, heavier material. Our wrought iron page covers that option in detail.
Can an Aluminum Fence Be Customized?
Yes. Standard designs come in many colors and styles, and posts, balusters, and heights can be tailored. Flat-top, picket, three-rail, and arched designs are the most common starting points.
Does Aluminum Work on a Sloped Yard?
It does. Aluminum adapts to uneven ground more easily than some other materials, which makes it a common pick for lots with a grade. A site visit shows how the sections should follow your ground.
Can Aluminum Fencing Go on Commercial Properties?
Yes. Big Easy Fence installs aluminum fencing for both residential and commercial properties in New Orleans. Business projects are covered on the commercial metal fences page.
