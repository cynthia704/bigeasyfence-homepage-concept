What Walker property owners ask before choosing chain link.

6 Common QuestionsStraight answers
How Long Does Chain Link Installation Take in Walker?
Our Walker page puts the average at two to three days, depending on the area to be fenced. The first day usually goes to digging and setting the concrete posts, and the next day the mesh goes up.
How Much Does Chain Link Fencing Cost in Walker?
Our Walker page gives an average of $2,193 for a residential chain link fence. Height, gauge, coating, and the length of the run all change the total. Request a free quote and we will price your project.
What Coatings Are Available for Chain Link?
Galvanized zinc is the economical choice and resists rust and corrosion. Vinyl over the core wire adds a strong layer that resists cracking and peeling. Polyester and PVC coatings offer natural colors to blend with your property.
Is a Chain Link Fence Low Maintenance?
Yes. Galvanized chain link is easy to clean and maintain. If you would rather leave it to someone else, Big Easy Fence offers chain link maintenance services in Walker.
Do You Install Chain Link for Businesses in Walker?
Yes. We install chain link fences and gates for homes and for commercial properties, including schools and other public places. The same fence can be scaled to the size of the site.
Can I Get a Stronger Chain Link Fence for Security?
Yes. A heavier gauge fence with a larger wire diameter is much stronger, and you can choose the height and color to suit the property and the purpose of the fence.
