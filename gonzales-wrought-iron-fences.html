Property Lines

Iron Fences as Property Boundaries

A few specifics make a boundary fence work.

3 Key PointsWhat to expect
01

Match Your Setting

In a big city, a more modern design fits. In a rural area, an antique or Victorian style suits the surroundings.

02

Pick the Right Height

For a boundary fence, choose one at least 6 feet tall. That height is enough to set your property apart from the next one. Check Gonzales and parish rules on fence height before you commit.

03

Mind the Gaps

To keep pedestrians off your property, choose a fence with openings no more than 4 inches wide. Gaps that narrow keep most people from passing through.

Gonzales Wrought Iron Fence Questions, Answered

What Gonzales property owners ask before choosing iron.

6 Common QuestionsStraight answers
How Much Does a Wrought Iron Fence Cost in Gonzales?
It depends on the design, the length and height of the fence, and the labor. Custom ornamental work costs more than a simple design. A free estimate tells you what your Gonzales fence will cost before you commit.
What Styles of Wrought Iron Fence Are Available?
Picket, rail, ornamental, and post and rail styles. Picket fences come in Victorian, Craftsman, and Colonial looks, and rail fences come in two-rail or three-rail designs.
How Tall Should a Boundary Fence Be?
For a property boundary, choose a fence at least 6 feet tall. It is enough to separate your property from others. Check local height rules for Gonzales before you build.
How Wide Can the Gaps Be?
To keep pedestrians off your property, choose openings no more than 4 inches wide. Narrower gaps make it hard for people to pass through.
Can You Design a Custom Iron Fence for My Gonzales Home?
Yes. Big Easy Fence can custom-design a wrought iron fence for your specific needs, in styles and colors that suit a home or a business.
Which Iron Style Fits a Rural or City Property?
In a big city, go for a more modern look. In a rural area, an antique or Victorian style suits the setting. Either way, the design can be customized to your requirements.
