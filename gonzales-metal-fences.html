Aluminum, steel, and wrought iron are the main choices.

Lightweight, durable, and relatively inexpensive. Aluminum does not rust or corrode, so you never have to paint it to protect it.

02

Steel

Extremely strong and durable, and good value for the money. A protective finish helps keep rust away.

Very strong and long-lasting, and popular with homeowners for its beauty and timeless look.

Gates and Security

Metal Gates and Security

A fence is only as good as its gate, and metal handles both.

3 Key PointsWhat to expect
01

Metal Gates for Fences

Metal gates keep people and animals out of an area, or keep them in. They can be welded to a metal fence or mounted on hardware that lets them stand alone. They also work well around a backyard pool.

02

Sturdy Enough for Farm and Ranch

Metal gates hold up under the weight of an animal better than wood, which makes them a solid choice for farm and ranch fencing.

03

Deterring Trespassers

Most metal fences are hard to climb, so they discourage casual trespassers. Styles and heights vary widely, which lets you choose a fence that fits your security needs and your property.

Good to Know

Gonzales Metal Fence Questions, Answered

What Gonzales property owners ask about metal fencing.

6 Common QuestionsStraight answers
What Types of Metal Fences Can You Install in Gonzales?
The main options are aluminum, steel, and wrought iron. Aluminum is light and low-maintenance, steel is very strong, and wrought iron is prized for its look. We can help you match one to your Gonzales property.
How Much Does a Metal Fence Cost in Gonzales?
Our Gonzales page puts a simple low steel or aluminum fence at around $35 per foot, with about $5 more per linear foot for a color other than black, white, or gray. Your location, the amount of material, and other costs shift the final price, so ask for a free estimate.
What Colors Do Metal Fences Come In?
Common finishes include black, bronze, brown, green, gray, and silver. A metal fence can also be painted to suit your style.
Are Metal Fences Good for Security?
Yes. Most metal fences are difficult to climb, which deters casual trespassers. A properly designed and installed fence gives you physical and visual security, and you can pick a style and height that suits your home.
Can You Install Metal Gates in Gonzales?
Yes. Metal gates can be welded to a metal fence or hung on hardware that lets them stand alone. They are sturdy enough for farm and ranch use and work well around a pool.
Does Aluminum Fencing Need Painting?
No. Aluminum does not rust or corrode, so you never have to paint it to protect it. That is one reason it is a popular, low-upkeep choice.
