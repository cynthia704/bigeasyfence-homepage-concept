About Walker

Walker is a city in Livingston Parish, roughly 20 miles east of Baton Rouge. It had 6,374 residents at the 2020 census. Walker and Denham Springs are the only municipalities in the parish classified as cities.

A Short History of Walker

Michael Joseph Milton Jr. founded the community in 1825, and its first post office opened in 1856 as Milton Old Field. In 1890 it was renamed for Dr. William Elliott Walker, a legislator from nearby Springfield. Walker became a city in 2011.

Things to Do in Walker
  • Walker High School: The older school building, shown here.
  • Livingston Parish Literacy and Technology Center: Named for Sally Clausen and her brother, Thomas G. Clausen, former state education leaders.
Fun Facts About Walker
  • The old name "Old Field" refers to the open, cleared land that suited grazing cattle and early community gatherings.
