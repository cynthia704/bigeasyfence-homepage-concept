What Goes Into Your Outdoor Kitchen

Planning and budgeting for an outdoor kitchen isn't easy — here are the essential features we help you think through.

4 Essential ComponentsWhat we help you plan

The Grill

Your grill is the centerpiece of your outdoor kitchen — propane, electric, or charcoal, depending on your energy source and cooking preferences.

Flooring

Concrete is budget-friendly but can crack over time. Tiles look great but can crack in New Orleans' varying weather. Pavers are increasingly the preferred choice — robust, slip-resistant, and easy to replace if damaged.

Countertops

We'll help you select the right material for outdoor conditions — limestone, travertine, granite, or stainless steel.

Furniture

To create an inviting space for entertaining, we offer outdoor furniture options from sofas and lounges to dining chairs, tailored to your style and budget.
