One fence style serves very different properties. These are the three uses that come up most.

01

Wire and Finish

Galvanized or Vinyl-Coated Chain Link

The wire finish decides how the fence looks and how it handles the weather.

2 Key PointsWhat to expect
01

Galvanized Steel Wire

Standard chain link is steel wire with a zinc galvanized coating. The zinc slows rust and corrosion, which matters in hot, humid summers and stormy weather. The finish is easy to clean and does not need paint.

02

Vinyl-Coated Color Options

A vinyl coating over the wire adds color, commonly black or green, so the fence blends into landscaping or a building. Commercial and industrial sites can also add hardware along the top of the fence for extra security.

Good to Know

Baton Rouge Chain Link Fence Questions, Answered

What Baton Rouge property owners ask about chain link fencing.

6 Common QuestionsStraight answers
Is Chain Link a Good Choice for a Baton Rouge Home?
It can be. Chain link is affordable, sturdy, and low upkeep, and it works well around a backyard, garden, or pool area. If privacy matters, ask about slats or consider another material.
How Does Chain Link Hold Up in Baton Rouge Weather?
Galvanized steel wire is coated to resist rust and corrosion, which helps in a hot, humid climate with the occasional severe storm. Like any fence, it lasts longest when you check the posts, wire, and gates once in a while.
Can Chain Link Work for a Business or Industrial Site?
Yes. Warehouses, manufacturing plants, construction sites, and commercial lots all use it. Height can be tailored to the site, and added security options are available.
What Heights and Colors Are Available?
Height, color, and style can all be customized. Galvanized wire has a silver-gray look, and vinyl-coated wire adds color.
Does Chain Link Need Painting or Staining?
No. Galvanized steel does not need either one, and an occasional cleaning is usually all the upkeep it asks for.
