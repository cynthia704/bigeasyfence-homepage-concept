Good to Know

Prairieville Metal Fence Questions, Answered

What Prairieville property owners ask about metal fencing.

6 Common QuestionsStraight answers
How Much Does a Metal Fence Cost in Prairieville?
For a 4-foot-tall fence, our Prairieville page lists averages of $8 to $12 per linear foot for aluminum, $7 to $10 for steel, $25 to $30 for wrought iron, and $10 for corrugated metal. The style, the amount of fence, labor, and accessories move the final number.
Which Metal Is Best Around a Pool?
Aluminum. It is rust-proof, strong, durable, and low maintenance, and we highly recommend it for Prairieville properties with a swimming pool.
Should I Choose Aluminum or Steel?
Aluminum is the rust-proof, low-upkeep option, offered in picket or solid panel styles. Ornamental steel adds elegance, value, and security to a home or business. We offer free consultations to help you decide.
Can You Build a Custom Iron Fence in Prairieville?
Yes. Our installers can design a handcrafted custom iron fence to your specifications. It works with a wide range of architectural styles and combines decorative and safety features.
What Gates Can You Install With a Metal Fence?
We install automatic gates, childproof gates, and pedestrian gates, along with openers and decorative elements for wrought iron, steel, and custom iron fences.
How Do I Care for a Wrought Iron Fence?
Watch for rust and damage, and deal with it quickly. Spraying on rust-inhibiting paint each year helps a wrought iron fence last.
