Before You Decide

What Actually Changes Between Fence Types

The material you pick affects more than looks — here's what actually shifts, and what we walk every customer through.

4 Key FactorsWhat to weigh first

Climate & Weather

New Orleans humidity, heat, and storm season affect how different materials hold up over time — some fare much better than others.

Maintenance Tolerance

Wood needs the occasional stain or seal to hold up. Vinyl and aluminum need almost none.

Privacy vs. Visibility

Solid wood and vinyl fully close off a yard. Chain link, aluminum, and wrought iron stay open.

Budget & Lifespan

Upfront cost and how many years a fence lasts don't always move together — we'll walk you through both before you decide.
