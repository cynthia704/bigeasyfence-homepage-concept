Comparison

How Aluminum Compares With Iron and Steel

Each metal has its place. Here is how they differ in everyday use.

01

Aluminum and Wrought Iron

Iron is heavier and needs watching for rust once the finish wears through. Aluminum offers a similar ornamental look with less rust concern.

02

Aluminum and Steel

Steel is generally stronger and heavier, so it is often chosen where maximum strength matters. Aluminum is lighter and resists rust, which suits pools and decorative boundaries.

03

Picking by Purpose

Choose aluminum for looks and low upkeep, and steel where extra strength is the priority. A consultation helps match the metal to your property.

Good to Know

Abita Springs Aluminum Fence Questions, Answered

What Abita Springs owners ask about aluminum.

6 Common QuestionsStraight answers
Does an Aluminum Fence Rust in Abita Springs Weather?
Aluminum resists rust, unlike iron, so humidity and rain are less of a worry. It still benefits from an occasional rinse to keep dirt off the finish.
Do Aluminum Fences Need Painting or Staining?
No. Aluminum does not need the regular repainting or staining that wood asks for. Occasional cleaning is the main upkeep.
Is Aluminum a Good Choice Around a Pool?
Yes. Aluminum pool fencing adds a safety barrier while keeping a stylish look, and its rust resistance suits the damp conditions near a pool. Check local pool barrier rules for height and gate requirements.
What Aluminum Styles Are Available in Abita Springs?
Big Easy Fence offers styles from classic to modern. You can specify the style, height, and design that fit your Abita Springs property.
Can Aluminum Work for a Business Property?
Yes. Aluminum security fencing deters unauthorized access, and a fence with an entrance gate gives clients and visitors a professional first impression. Businesses in Abita Springs can use it for both purposes.
How Is Aluminum Different From Wrought Iron?
Iron is heavier and can rust once its finish wears through, while aluminum resists rust and asks for less upkeep. Both give an ornamental look, so the choice often comes down to strength and maintenance.
