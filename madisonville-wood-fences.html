Common Woods

Everyday Wood Options for Madisonville Fences

These four woods run from budget-friendly to sturdy and traditional.

01

Spruce

One of the most common fence woods. It comes in board-on-board, picket, and privacy styles, and the privacy style uses slats that block the view while letting air move through.

02

Pine

A budget-friendly choice for anyone who would rather not pay for cedar or cypress. It looks good and costs less, though it is not as durable as the pricier woods.

03

Fir

A durable wood that needs minimal maintenance and works in many kinds of fence projects. White fir costs more than pine.

04

White Oak

Rich, traditional appeal, and more durable than pine. A good pick if you want the classic look many people expect from a wood fence.

Planning Ahead

Choosing a Fence and Knowing How Long It Lasts

Two questions shape most wood fence projects: what the fence needs to do, and how long you want it to last.

2 Key PointsWhat to expect
01

What Your Fence Needs to Do

Start with the job. Do you want a privacy barrier, security as well, or something that does more than decorate? Then look at how much space you have. Those answers narrow down the material and style quickly.

02

How Long Wood Fences Last

With proper care and maintenance, most wood fences last about 20 years before they need major repairs or replacement. Installation quality, the wood you choose, and weather conditions all affect how soon a fence shows its age.

Natural Favorites

Cedar, Cypress, and Redwood

Three woods stand out for their natural resistance and their looks.

01

Cedar

Popular because it resists rot and insects. It can fade if left unstained or untreated, and it needs some upkeep over time.

02

Cypress

Strong and durable, and it holds up against termites. That combination makes it a favorite for fencing.

03

Redwood

An all-natural look that takes stain or paint well. With proper care, a redwood fence lasts a long time.

Good to Know

Madisonville Wood Fence Questions, Answered

What Madisonville homeowners and business owners ask about wood fences.

6 Common QuestionsStraight answers
What Wood Options Are Available in Madisonville?
Big Easy Fence works with cedar, cypress, pine, and more, including spruce, fir, white oak, and redwood. Each one differs in price, durability, and upkeep, so the right pick depends on what your fence needs to do.
Which Wood Is the Most Budget-Friendly?
Pine is the budget-friendly choice for Madisonville homeowners who do not want to spend on cedar or cypress. It is attractive and affordable, though it is not as durable. Fir costs more than pine, and white oak is more durable than pine.
How Long Does a Wood Fence Last?
With proper care and maintenance, most wood fences last about 20 years before major repairs or replacement. Installation quality, the wood, and weather conditions all change that timeline.
Does Cedar Need Staining?
Cedar resists rot and insects, but it can fade if it is not stained or treated. It also needs some upkeep over time, so plan on a regular maintenance routine.
How Do I Choose the Right Fence for My Madisonville Property?
Decide first whether you need privacy, security, or both, and whether the fence has to do more than decorate. Then consider how much space you have. Those answers point you toward the right material and style.
Do You Build Wood Fences for Businesses?
Yes. Big Easy Fence installs wood fences for homes and businesses in the Greater Madisonville area. Wood looks good on a property of any size.
