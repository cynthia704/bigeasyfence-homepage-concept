About Prairieville

Prairieville is a census-designated place in Ascension Parish, south of Baton Rouge and north of Gonzales. It is served by Highway 73 and Airline Highway and had 33,197 residents at the 2020 census.

A Short History of Prairieville

Prairieville started as a quiet community with few residents. It has grown into an expanding suburb of Baton Rouge as people moved out from the city.

Things to Do in Prairieville
  • Robert Penn Warren House: A historic home.
  • Phillips Farm: Another local landmark.
Fun Facts About Prairieville
  • If it were incorporated, it would be the largest city in Ascension Parish.
  • Its population is larger than Donaldsonville and Gonzales combined.
