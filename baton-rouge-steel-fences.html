Good to Know

Baton Rouge Steel Fence Questions, Answered

Common questions from Baton Rouge homeowners and businesses.

6 Common QuestionsStraight answers
Does Steel Rust in Baton Rouge Weather?
Bare steel can rust, and humidity speeds it up. A protective finish keeps moisture off the metal, so check for chips and touch them up early.
Is Steel Stronger Than Aluminum?
Generally yes. Steel is heavier and stands up better to hard impact, while aluminum is lighter and does not rust. Which one fits better depends on the property, and a consultation can help you compare.
Can Steel Fences Be Decorative?
Yes. Steel can carry scrollwork, finials, and other detail, or it can stay plain and modern.
Do You Install Steel Fences for Businesses in Baton Rouge?
Yes. Retail storefronts, warehouses, manufacturing plants, and construction sites are all common uses, and the height, style, and finish can be tailored to the site.
What Upkeep Does a Steel Fence Need?
Occasional cleaning and a look over the finish. Fix chips before rust gets started and the fence stays in good shape.
Can I Choose the Height and Finish for My Baton Rouge Fence?
Yes. Height, style, and finish can all be customized to suit your property and your personal taste.
