Enhancing Home Security With Automatic Gate Fencing: A Reliable Choice

An automatic gate adds a layer of security at the driveway and takes the daily chore of opening and closing out of your hands. This page covers how the gates operate, the main types, material choices, and what solar power can and cannot do.

Why Automate

Why Choose an Automatic Gate in New Orleans

The gate is often the first layer of security a property has, and automation makes that layer easier to live with.

5 Key BenefitsWhat an automatic gate delivers

Controlled Access

A closed gate keeps unauthorized visitors off the driveway, which gives you peace of mind even when you are away from home.

Child and Pet Safety

Automatic gates reduce the chance that children or pets open the gate and wander out.

Everyday Convenience

You never have to wonder whether the gate got closed. It opens and closes on command, from the car or the house.

Custom Looks

Gates can be designed to match the style of your property, so security adds to curb appeal instead of taking from it.

Fit for Any Property Size

Automatic driveway gates used to belong to estates and ranches. Today they suit homes and businesses of nearly any size.

Gate Types

Types of Automatic Gates

Gates are grouped by how they move. The right one depends on your driveway, your space, and how you plan to use it.

01

Sliding Gates

The gate rolls sideways along the fence line. Variations include V track, rear pipe, and cantilever designs, and this style suits driveways with room alongside the opening.

02

Swing Gates

Single swing gates use one leaf, and double swing gates use two that meet in the middle. Uphill swing designs handle sloped driveways.

03

Vertical Pivot and Lift and Swing

These gates rise or lift as they open, which is useful when a sideways or outward swing does not fit the space.

04

Underground Systems

The operating hardware sits below grade, out of sight, for a cleaner look at the gate.

05

Barrier and RV Gates

Barrier arms control vehicle access, and RV gates are sized for taller, wider vehicles.

06

Entry Controls

Keypad entry, card-based entry, and remotes decide who gets in and how, and they can be paired with almost any gate style.

How It Works

How Automatic Gates Operate

A plain-language overview. Gate installation is work for trained professionals.

3 Key PointsWhat to expect
01

A Solid Foundation

The gate hangs from or rides beside sturdy posts, pillars, or columns, usually concrete, brick, or stone, chosen to carry the weight of the gate. Heavier gates need stronger supports, which is one reason the planning stage matters.

02

The Opener and Its Power

An automatic opener is attached to the gate and its post and connected to a power source. The opener includes a rechargeable battery that runs the opening and closing motion, and some setups run on solar power.

03

Ways to Open the Gate

Remotes and keypads are the most common ways to operate a gate, with card-based entry and more advanced options available too. Storm season makes it worth asking how a gate behaves during a power outage.

Materials

Material Options for Your Automatic Gate

Each material has trade-offs. The right pick blends with your property and suits the way the gate will be used.

01

Aluminum

Lightweight, which is easy on the opener, though it is susceptible to dents.

02

Steel

Low maintenance and highly durable, but heavy, so it needs strong supports and a suitable opener.

03

Wrought Iron

Decorative and versatile, though it generally costs more than other options.

04

Wood

Strong and attractive, but it takes high maintenance, which is a real consideration in humid New Orleans weather.

05

Vinyl

Inexpensive, though prone to denting.

Solar Power

Exploring Solar-Powered Automatic Gates

Solar is an eco-friendly alternative to conventional power, and it is especially suited to vacation homes and rarely used entrances.

01

Renewable Energy

Solar power is renewable and produces fewer emissions than conventional electricity.

02

Lower Running Costs

A solar gate removes ongoing electricity costs for the opener and reduces the electrical work the site needs.

03

Help on Long Driveways

Where the gate sits far from the house, solar avoids running power a long way. Panels mounted on or near the gate recharge the battery.

04

Works Through Outages

Because the opener runs on a battery, the gate can keep operating during a power outage. Heavy use or long stretches of low light can drain it, so size the system to your traffic.

Good to Know

Automatic Gate Questions, Answered

What New Orleans homeowners ask before adding a gate.

6 Common QuestionsStraight answers
What Types of Automatic Gates Are Available?
Gates are classified by how they move: sliding, swing, vertical pivot, lift and swing, and underground systems, plus barrier and RV gates. Uphill swing designs handle sloped driveways, and entry can be controlled by remote, keypad, or card.
How Does an Automatic Gate Work?
The gate is supported by sturdy posts, pillars, or columns, and an opener attached to the gate and post moves it open and closed. The opener draws on a power source and includes a rechargeable battery, and you trigger it with a remote, keypad, or another entry method.
Can an Automatic Gate Run on Solar Power?
Yes. Solar-powered openers recharge from panels and suit long driveways and rarely used properties. They can keep working through an outage on battery power, though heavy use or several dim days can drain the battery.
Which Gate Material Is Best in New Orleans?
It depends on the look, the budget, and how much upkeep you want. Steel and aluminum are low-maintenance choices, wrought iron is the most decorative, and wood needs the most care in humid weather. Steel is heavy, so it calls for stronger supports.
Do Automatic Gates Improve Security?
They do. A gate that closes on its own keeps unauthorized people off the driveway and reduces the chance that children or pets let themselves out. It also removes the worry about whether the gate was left open.
Should I Install an Automatic Gate Myself?
Opener kits are sold at home improvement stores, but a gate involves heavy moving parts, sturdy supports, and electrical connections. A professional installer is the safer route, and it helps the gate operate smoothly for years.
