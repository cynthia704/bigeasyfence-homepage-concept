Coatings and Care

Keeping a Steel Fence in Good Shape

Steel is tough, and its finish does much of the work.

2 Key PointsWhat to expect
01

Why the Coating Matters

Bare steel rusts when moisture reaches it, so the finish is the fence's first line of defense. Abita Springs sees hot summers and heavy rain, which puts that coating to the test.

02

Simple Upkeep

Look the fence over now and then for chips or scratches in the finish, and have them touched up before rust takes hold. Occasional cleaning keeps the color fresh.
