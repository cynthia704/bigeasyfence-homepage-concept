Why Wood

Why Sorrento Homeowners Choose Wood Fencing

Wood has been used for fencing for centuries, and it is still popular with homeowners today.

2 Key PointsWhat to expect
01

Natural Beauty and Privacy

A well-kept wood fence adds character to a yard, and homeowners like its rustic look next to chain link or other modern materials. Wood posts give a quaint feel while providing the privacy many people want.

02

Upkeep to Expect

Wood asks for regular care. Oiling and staining keep it looking its best. Cost sits in the moderate to expensive range, and labor runs higher than it does for vinyl.

Materials

Cedar, Cypress, and Redwood Fences

Cedar is the most common wood for fencing, with two other favorites close behind.

01

Cedar

Naturally water-resistant, so it does not need constant painting. It is also less prone to cracking and splitting than some other woods, which makes repairs easier.

02

Cypress

A beautiful, water-resistant wood that is also insect-proof, which helps keep out termites and other common pests.

03

Redwood

Another attractive, water-resistant choice that suits a traditional yard.

Good to Know

Sorrento Wood Fence Questions, Answered

What Sorrento homeowners ask about wood fences.

6 Common QuestionsStraight answers
What Wood Is Most Common for Sorrento Fences?
Cedar is the most common wood used in fencing. Cypress and redwood are the other popular choices, and all three are water-resistant.
Does a Wood Fence Need Maintenance?
Yes. Regular oiling and staining keep a wood fence looking its best. That upkeep is the trade-off for the natural look wood gives a Sorrento yard.
Can You Build a Custom Wood Fence in Sorrento?
Yes. Big Easy Fence builds custom wood fences for homes of all shapes and sizes, and our technicians work with you to get exactly what you want.
Do You Repair or Replace Old Wood Fences?
Both. If you have an existing fence you want to update, we handle repair and replacement as well as new installation.
Why Hire a Contractor Instead of Building It Myself?
Building a wood fence is tedious and time-consuming, and many people lack the tools or experience for it. A contractor can also suggest options you may not have considered.
Do You Build Commercial Wood Fences?
Yes. Big Easy Fence offers both residential and commercial wood fencing for Sorrento properties.
