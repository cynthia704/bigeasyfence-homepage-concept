What We Inspect

What We Check Before Any Repair Starts

A fence rarely has just one problem, so the evaluation covers the whole run. These are the issues we look for.

6 Warning SignsChecked at every evaluation

Rot

Soft, dark, or crumbling wood, usually near the ground and around posts. Rot keeps spreading, so the earlier it is found, the smaller the repair.

Termite Damage

Hollowed or chewed wood that can still look sound on the surface. Termites weaken boards and posts from the inside.

Loose Panels

Panels that have pulled away from their rails or posts and shift or rattle when the wind picks up.

Broken Posts

A cracked or leaning post puts strain on every panel attached to it, so it is one of the first things we check.

Rust and Bent Metal

Rust on posts, rails, and hardware, along with bent or broken sections on metal and chain-link fences.

Warping and Wear

Boards that have twisted or bowed, plus cosmetic damage that hurts how the fence looks from the street or the yard.

Fence Types

Repairs for Every Fence Material

The right fix depends on what the fence is made of. Here is what we handle in Covington.

01

Wood Fences

Damaged panels, boards, and pickets get repaired or replaced, along with problems from rot, termites, or warping. New material is matched to the existing fence so the repair blends in.

02

Vinyl Fences

Cracks, holes, and loose sections on privacy, semi-privacy, picket, and ranch rail styles. The aim is a fence that looks the way it did when it was installed.

03

Metal and Chain-Link

Bent or broken sections, damaged posts, and rusted parts. These fences are durable, but time and weather still wear them down, and repaired sections should match the rest.

Good to Know

Covington Fence Repair Questions, Answered

What Covington property owners ask before calling for a repair.

6 Common QuestionsStraight answers
What Fence Materials Do You Repair in Covington?
Wood, vinyl, metal, and chain-link. Wood repairs cover panels, boards, pickets, rot, termite damage, and warping. Vinyl repairs cover cracks, holes, and loose sections, and metal and chain-link repairs cover bent sections, damaged posts, and rust.
What Happens During the Fence Assessment?
We inspect the fence for rot, termite damage, loose panels, broken posts, rust, and any other structural or cosmetic problems. The findings shape a repair plan built around your fence.
How Is New Material Matched to an Old Fence?
On wood fences, replacement boards and panels are chosen to match the existing ones so the repair blends into the design. Repaired sections of metal and vinyl fences are matched to the existing fence in the same way.
Do You Repair Commercial Fences in Covington?
Yes. Big Easy Fence repairs fences on both residential and commercial properties in Covington.
How Long Will My Repair Take?
The goal is to finish within a timeframe agreed with you before work begins, with minimal disruption to your routine. The actual timeline depends on what the evaluation turns up.
When Should I Call About a Damaged Fence?
Sooner is better. Loose panels, leaning posts, rust, rot, and missing boards tend to get worse over time, and a fence with gaps gives less security and privacy. If you see any of them, schedule an evaluation.
