About Gonzales

Gonzales is a city in Ascension Parish, near the Mississippi River, with 12,231 residents at the 2020 census. It is known as the "Jambalaya Capital of the World."

A Short History of Gonzales

Gonzales was laid out and subdivided in 1906 and incorporated as a village in 1922. Governor John J. McKeithen declared it the Jambalaya Capital of the World in 1968.

Things to Do in Gonzales
  • Jambalaya Festival: The annual event behind the city's nickname.
  • Historic Buildings: Landmarks include the Gonzales Post Office and the former Louisiana Railway and Navigation Company Station.
Fun Facts About Gonzales
  • The Jambalaya Capital title was made official by a governor in 1968.
