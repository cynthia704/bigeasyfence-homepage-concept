Fence Posts

How We Read the Damage on a Fence Post

Posts hold everything up, so a bad one gets checked first. Here is how the decision usually goes.

4 Post ChecksWhat gets assessed

Check the Condition

The first question is whether the post is still sound. Rot, cracks, and breaks all show up on inspection.

Rotten or Broken Posts

A post that has rotted through or broken needs to be replaced. There is nothing solid left to reinforce.

Sound but Shaky Posts

A post in good condition can often be saved by resetting it in concrete or reinforcing it with a steel post.

Check What Surrounds It

The fencing next to a post gets checked too, since a failing post often puts stress on the panels and rails around it.

What We Repair

Gonzales Fence Repairs by Material

The repair depends on what your fence is made of.

01

Wood Fences

Replacing a whole panel is one option, and swapping only the broken boards is another. Replacement boards should match the width and thickness of the originals, and damaged brackets can be replaced too.

02

Vinyl Fences

Broken pieces can be bonded with vinyl adhesive, and holes or cracks can be patched with vinyl sealant. When the damage is extensive, whole sections may need replacing.

03

Metal Fences

A hole can be patched with metal. A sagging fence can be reinforced with a metal brace along the inside of the fence line, at the top, the bottom, or one end.

04

Chain-Link and Iron

Big Easy Fence installs and repairs chain-link and iron fences as well as vinyl and wood, so mixed-material properties can be handled in one place.

Gate Repair

Gate Repair: Fix It or Replace It

How much damage the gate has decides the repair. Three outcomes are common.

3 Key PointsWhat to expect
01

Hinges and Latches

Sometimes the gate itself is fine and the trouble sits at the hinges or the latch. Fixing the hardware is the quickest route back to a gate that swings and closes properly.

02

A Few Bad Boards

If only a couple of boards are warped or broken, replacing them is usually cheaper and easier than replacing the gate. New boards get a proper fit, which keeps the gate looking good longer.

03

When the Whole Gate Goes

With extensive damage, or serious trouble in the hinges and latch system, replacing the entire gate is the better call. A replacement wood gate should be finished with a protective sealer or paint so it holds up.

Gonzales Fence Repair Questions, Answered

Common questions from Gonzales property owners.

6 Common QuestionsStraight answers
How Much Does Fence Repair Cost in Gonzales?
It depends on the size of the fence, the type of fence, and how severe the damage is. A few loose boards cost far less than a leaning run of posts or a failed gate. A free estimate gives you the real number for your fence.
When Does a Fence Post Need Replacing?
When it is rotten or broken. A post in good condition can often be reinforced instead, for example with a steel post or by setting it firmly in concrete. Either way, the fencing around the post gets checked for damage too.
Can a Damaged Gate Be Repaired Instead of Replaced?
Often, yes. If the problem is the hinges or the latch, or a few boards are warped or broken, a repair is usually cheaper and easier. If the damage is extensive or the hinge and latch system has major problems, a new gate is the better fix.
What Can Be Done About a Sagging Metal Fence in Gonzales?
A metal brace along the inside of the fence line supports the fence and stops the sag. Braces can run along the top and bottom or sit at one end, and holes in the metal can be patched.
How Are Cracks in a Vinyl Fence Repaired?
Broken vinyl pieces can be bonded back together with vinyl adhesive, and holes or cracks can be patched with vinyl sealant. If the damage is widespread, whole sections may need replacing. A repair crew can tell you which applies to your fence.
Which Fence Types Do You Repair in Gonzales, LA?
Wood, vinyl, metal, chain-link, and iron fences, plus gates and posts. Big Easy Fence also installs these fence styles, so a repair estimate can sit alongside an installation quote if you need both.
