Which Metal

Choosing a Metal for Your Baton Rouge Fence

Each metal suits a different job. Here is how the three compare at a glance.

01

Steel

Very strong, and a common pick for security and commercial perimeters. It needs a protective finish, and chips in the coating should be touched up before rust can start.

02

Aluminum

Light and rust-free, which suits humid weather. Its open picket styles are a popular choice for yards, pool areas, and business frontage.

03

Wrought Iron

Handcrafted and highly ornamental, with scrollwork and finials. It asks for the most care, because iron rusts once its paint wears through.

Upkeep

What Metal Fences Ask of You

Metal keeps upkeep light. A few habits keep it that way.

01

Clean It

Rinse off dirt and debris from time to time so the finish stays sharp.

02

Inspect It

Look over posts, welds, gates, and hardware, and check for chips in the finish.

03

Touch Up Chips

Steel and iron rust where bare metal shows, so repair scratches early. Aluminum does not rust, though it still benefits from a rinse.

04

No Pest Worries

Termites and rot cannot damage metal, which spares you the staining and board repairs that wood asks for.

Good to Know

Baton Rouge Metal Fence Questions, Answered

What Baton Rouge property owners ask about metal fencing.

6 Common QuestionsStraight answers
Which Metal Is Best for a Baton Rouge Fence?
It depends on the job. Steel is very strong, aluminum does not rust, and wrought iron is the most ornamental. A consultation compares them against your property, your goals, and your budget.
Do Metal Fences Need a Lot of Upkeep?
Very little. Steel and iron need a sound finish, so touch up chips before rust starts. Aluminum needs mostly cleaning. Metal will not rot, and termites cannot damage it.
Can a Metal Fence Give Privacy?
Yes. Slats, panels, and other privacy features can be added to the design, and they can also help cut some street noise.
Can You Customize a Metal Fence for My Baton Rouge Property?
Yes. Patterns, ornamental accents, finishes, and height can all be tailored to your property and its architecture.
Do You Install Metal Fences for Businesses in Baton Rouge?
Yes. Metal fencing serves both homes and businesses, including commercial lots and industrial yards where gates and security matter.
How Long Does a Metal Fence Last?
Many years, as long as the finish is sound and gets regular care. Metal is not affected by rot or pests, but bare spots and chips need attention.
