6 Common QuestionsStraight answers
How Much Does a Wrought Iron Fence Cost in Denham Springs?
Intricate designs, decorative additions, a gate, height, and the size of the area all push the price up, and a simple, shorter fence costs less. A free quote gives you the number for your fence.
Can You Add a Gate to a Wrought Iron Fence?
Yes. Any custom iron fence can include a matching gate, and you can choose from a gallery of designs. Add-ons include a wireless gate bell, a magnetic latch, and a self-closing hinge.
Can a Wrought Iron Fence Be Repaired in Denham Springs?
Yes. Iron can rust and weaken with weather changes or take damage, and repairs restore the fence. The gallery shows repairs completed for other iron fences.
How Does Iron Compare With Chain Link?
Chain link is popular with clients, but wrought iron offers more security and lasts longer. It can also be customized to look far more decorative.
Can I Choose a Color Other Than Black?
Yes. Iron fences come in a range of colors besides black, so the fence can match the overall design of your Denham Springs home. Curved bars, custom post caps, and decorative shapes are options too.
Can You Restore a Victorian-Era Fence?
Wrought iron can be used to restore a fence from the Victorian era. Reach out with photos or details of your fence, and the team can talk through what the restoration would involve.
