About Mandeville

Mandeville is a city on the north shore of Lake Pontchartrain, south of Interstate 12 and directly across the lake from New Orleans. It had 13,192 residents at the 2020 census and is part of the New Orleans-Metairie metropolitan area.

A Short History of Mandeville

Mandeville was founded in 1834 and incorporated in 1840. It grew from a plantation established by Bernard de Marigny in the early 19th century and became a popular summer escape for New Orleanians looking to beat the heat.

Things to Do in Mandeville
  • Fontainebleau State Park: Lakeside state park on the north shore.
  • Seven Sisters Oak: A famous old oak tree.
  • Sunset Point Fishing Pier: A place to fish or watch the sun go down.
  • Dew Drop Jazz and Social Hall: A landmark of the area's jazz history.
Fun Facts About Mandeville
  • The city has a notable jazz history, with well-known musicians performing at venues around town.
