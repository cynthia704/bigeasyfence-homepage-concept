Wood Types

Wood Options for Walker Fences

Three woods cover the range from tight budgets to long-term durability.

01

Cedar

Cedar has a warm, rugged color and is the top pick for many clients. It is sensitive to moisture and can wear out in time, so ongoing maintenance helps it last.

02

Redwood

More expensive than other woods, and more durable. It resists termite damage and works as a low-maintenance fence material for a home.

03

Spruce

The lowest-cost wood, and a good fit when the budget is tight. Treating the wood and adding sealant protects it and improves its look.

Making a Wood Fence Last

With good maintenance, a wood fence can last up to 20 years. Repairs, staining, and painting on a regular schedule keep it in shape. The wood is treated against moisture and finished with a UV-protective coat to help it hold its color.

Walker Wood Fence Questions, Answered

What Walker homeowners ask about wood fences.

6 Common QuestionsStraight answers
How Much Does a Wood Fence Cost in Walker?
Our Walker page says most homeowners pay between $1,711 and $3,290, with prices running $12 to $27 per linear foot for labor and materials. The national average is $2,755. Your final price depends on the wood, labor, materials, fence size, and design.
How Long Does Wood Fence Installation Take in Walker?
A standard 150-foot fence takes about 2 days, and the average across jobs is 3 days. If the ground on your property is difficult, installation can take up to 5 days.
How Long Does Staining or Painting Take?
Staining or painting a wood fence takes around 2 days. It is a separate step from installation, so plan for it in your schedule.
Which Woods Can I Choose in Walker?
Cedar, redwood, and spruce are the options on our Walker page. Cedar has a warm color, redwood is more durable and pricier, and spruce is the lowest-cost choice.
How Long Will a Wood Fence Last?
A wood fence can last up to 20 years when it is properly maintained. Repairing, staining, and painting on a regular schedule are the steps that keep it going.
Do You Repair Wood Fences in Walker?
Yes. Along with installation, Big Easy Fence handles wood repairs and maintenance for Walker properties.
