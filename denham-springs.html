About Denham Springs

Denham Springs is a city in Livingston Parish, in the Florida Parishes and the Baton Rouge metropolitan area. It had 9,286 residents at the 2020 census and is the largest area of commercial and residential development in the parish.

A Short History of Denham Springs

Springs that surface at the base of a low ridge give the city its name. It was incorporated in 1903 and became a city in 1957. The railroad and the growth of Baton Rouge made it a bedroom community, and it became the banking and commercial center of Livingston Parish.

Things to Do in Denham Springs
  • Denham Springs Antique Village: The historic downtown, now a cultural arts district with antique shops, boutiques, restaurants, and cafes.
  • Denham Springs City Hall: Listed on the National Register of Historic Places in 1993.
Fun Facts About Denham Springs
  • The area was once known as Amite Springs and Hill's Springs.
  • Denham Springs sits where US 190 and Interstate 12 cross LA 16, the main north-south road in western Livingston Parish.
