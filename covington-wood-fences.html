Why Choose a Wood Fence in Covington

Is a wood fence worth the time, effort, and money? For homeowners who want more privacy, the answer is yes.

5 Key BenefitsWhat wood delivers

Privacy

The most obvious advantage of a wood fence. It separates your yard from your neighbor's and gives you space to relax.

Security and Safety

A wood fence improves the security of your home and keeps small children and pets from wandering off the property.

Out of Sight

A fence can hide unattractive utilities, such as trash bins and recycling containers, while adding to the look of your property.

Curb Appeal

Wood looks natural, and it can be stained or painted to match the exterior of your home.

Styles for Any Budget

Wood fences come in many styles and can be tailored to fit your budget.

Styles

Types of Wood Fences

Each type has its own advantages and drawbacks. These are the most popular.

01

Picket Fences

The best-known wood fence, typically made from cedar or pine. Pickets keep people and animals on your property while letting air and light pass through.

02

Rail Fences

A good match for large areas such as pastures and fields. They are made from long pieces of lumber connected together, and they are easy to repair if damaged. They also mark property boundaries well.

03

Privacy Fences

Ideal if you want a secluded home. They come in many styles and heights, and they give you room to relax without feeling watched by neighbors.

Materials

Best Types of Wood for Fences in Covington

Not all woods are equal. Some suit certain climates better than others.

01

Cypress

A popular choice for Covington fences. It is naturally rot-resistant and insect-proof, needs little upkeep, and is easy to clean with soap and water.

02

Cedar

Naturally resistant to rot, insects, and decay. It needs little upkeep, the grain looks good, and it takes stain in almost any color.

03

Redwood

A distinct, attractive grain, especially when stained, and naturally resistant to decay and rot.

04

Hem-Fir

Inexpensive and readily available. Hemlock grows fast and does not develop unsightly knotholes the way some woods do.

Care and Upkeep

How Long Wood Fences Last and How to Maintain Them

A little regular care goes a long way.

2 Key PointsWhat to expect
01

How Long a Wood Fence Lasts

Most wood fences last 10 to 15 years, and with consistent care they can reach 20. Stain or paint keeps the fence matched to your home. If yours is more than 15 years old, it is likely rotting or damaged in some way.

02

Simple Maintenance Tips

Check the posts for rot or damage and clear dirt from around them. Refresh old stain with paint made for wood fences. Keep mulch away from the posts, and trim tall grass near the fence without cutting into the bark of the posts. If you cannot restore it yourself, contact us for professional advice.

Covington Wood Fence Questions, Answered

What Covington homeowners ask before installing a wood fence.

6 Common QuestionsStraight answers
How Much Does a Wood Fence Cost in Covington?
Our Covington page puts the average installed cost between $5,793 and $6,697. The final price depends on the materials, the size of the area, the fence height, whether it is custom-made for your yard, whether the posts are set in concrete, and the type of wood. A free estimate gives you your real number.
How Long Does Wood Fence Installation Take?
It depends on the size and number of panels. A 4-foot picket fence and a 6-foot privacy fence take different amounts of work, but installation typically takes a day or two.
Are Wood Fences Worth It?
For homeowners who want privacy, yes. Wood looks good, separates one yard from another, hides things like trash bins, and helps keep children and pets on your property.
What Types of Wood Fences Are Popular?
Picket fences, rail fences, and privacy fences. Picket is the most traditional, rail suits large open areas and boundaries, and privacy fences suit anyone who wants a secluded yard.
How Long Do Wood Fences Last?
Typically 10 to 15 years, and up to 20 with proper care. Staining or painting on a regular schedule and checking the posts for rot helps a fence reach the top of that range.
Which Wood Is Best for a Covington Fence?
Cypress, cedar, redwood, and hem-fir are the common choices. Cypress and cedar are naturally resistant to rot and insects, redwood has a striking grain, and hem-fir is the budget-friendly option. We can help you compare them for your property.
