What to Know Before Choosing Wood

Wood has real strengths and a few limits worth planning for.

01

Regular Upkeep

Plan on painting or staining every few years and replacing boards as they wear out.

02

Weather Exposure

Rain, moisture, and severe weather are hard on wood, so protect the fence with a good finish. In high winds, tall solid fences take extra strain.

03

Rot and Water Damage

Boards can rot after repeated rainstorms when water is left to sit. Quick repairs stop small problems from spreading.

04

Lumber Choice

Cedar and redwood are the woods to look at first if you want a fence that lasts for many years.

6 Common QuestionsStraight answers
What Wood Is Best for a Donaldsonville Fence?
Cedar and redwood are the two woods to start with. Both protect well against rot and both look great, and they last for many years with care.
Can I Stain a Wood Fence in Donaldsonville?
Yes. Wood takes different stains and finishes, so you can pick a color that suits your house. Refinishing every few years also protects the boards.
What Are the Downsides of a Wood Fence?
Wood needs upkeep, including painting or staining every few years and replacing boards that rot or take water damage. Severe weather and high winds can also be tough on it.
Which Wood Fence Style Gives the Most Privacy?
A privacy fence, which comes in many styles and heights. Picket and rail styles let air and light through, so they give less cover.
