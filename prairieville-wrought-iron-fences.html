Why Iron

Why Choose a Wrought Iron Fence for Your Home

Iron costs more up front than many fence materials. The reasons it can still make sense are below.

5 Key ReasonsWhat iron delivers

Long-Run Value

The initial cost is higher than other fencing materials, but iron can pay off over time, which makes the upfront price easier to justify.

Nearly Unlimited Designs

Styles and patterns range from a simple picket look to a more complex hairpin style with ornamental extras.

Low Maintenance

An iron fence does not need painting or staining. The powder coat is applied to resist chipping and peeling, and maintenance service is available.

Durability

Iron stands up to harsh weather and sudden impact, so you spend less time worrying about the fence.

Contains Animals

The strength of iron makes it a strong choice for keeping pets and other animals where they belong.

Care and Upkeep

Keeping a Wrought Iron Fence in Shape

Three habits do most of the work.

3 Key PointsWhat to expect
01

Regular Cleaning

Cleaning an iron fence on a regular basis prevents many short-term problems. Maintenance service can keep it clean by removing caked-on debris before it causes further damage and more costly problems.

02

Regular Inspection

Routine inspections catch problems early. A maintenance check looks for worn paint and signs of cracking, and if multiple issues turn up, you get honest advice on whether replacement makes sense.

03

Prompt Rust Removal

Rust gets removed with sandpaper or a standard wire brush, and a protective anti-rust spray goes on to keep it from building up again. Quick action keeps a small spot from spreading.

Prairieville Wrought Iron Fence Questions, Answered

What Prairieville property owners ask before choosing iron.

6 Common QuestionsStraight answers
What Affects the Cost of a Wrought Iron Fence?
Materials, labor, tools, the area to be fenced, the size of the fence, and the color all play a part. Intricate designs and decorative additions push the price higher. A free estimate gives you real numbers.
Is a Wrought Iron Fence in Prairieville Worth the Price?
Iron costs more up front than many other materials, but it is durable and easy to maintain, so it can be the most cost-effective choice over the long run. Many Prairieville owners weigh that against the upfront price.
Does a Wrought Iron Fence Need Painting?
No painting or staining is needed on a Prairieville iron fence. The powder coat is applied to resist chipping and peeling, and maintenance service is available if you want the fence kept in top shape.
How Do You Deal With Rust on Iron?
Rust is removed with sandpaper or a wire brush, then the area is coated with a protective anti-rust spray. Catching rust early keeps repairs small.
Can You Match Gates and Railings to My Fence?
Yes. Big Easy Fence offers matching walk gates, iron gates, and iron railings, so the whole property has a cohesive look.
Do You Install Iron Fences for Businesses in Prairieville?
Yes. The team handles residential and commercial wrought iron fencing, with designs picked to fit your budget, structure, and style.
