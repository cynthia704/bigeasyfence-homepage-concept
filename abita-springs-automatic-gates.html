Gate Types

Swing Gates and Sliding Gates Compared

The first choice is how the gate moves. Your layout usually settles it.

2 Key PointsWhat to expect
01

Swing Gates

Panels pivot on hinges and open like a large door, inward or outward. They need clear space to swing, so the slope, the landscaping, and the distance from the street all matter.

02

Sliding Gates

The gate rolls sideways along the fence line. That suits tight driveways where there is no room to swing, provided a stretch of clear fence line sits beside the opening.

Access Options

How People and Vehicles Get In

The access method decides who can enter and how easily.

01

Remote Controls

A button on a remote opens and closes the gate without leaving your vehicle, which helps in bad weather and on late-night arrivals.

02

Keypads and Intercoms

Guests enter a code or call from the gate, so you decide when to let them in.

03

Access Control Systems

Business sites often use card or code systems so only authorized staff and visitors get through.

04

Pedestrian Gates

A walkway gate with access control lets authorized people in on foot while the driveway gate stays closed.

Safety and Backup

Safety Features and Backup Power

A moving gate needs protection built in. Ask about each of these when you compare options.

4 Things to AskBefore you choose

Obstruction Sensors

Sensors detect a person, pet, or vehicle in the gate's path and stop or reverse it.

Manual Release

A way to open the gate by hand when the power is out or the operator fails.

Backup Power

Battery backup can keep a gate working through an outage. Ask what the system you are considering supports.

Professional Installation

Gate operators involve electrical work and heavy moving parts. A professional installer is the safer route, and it keeps the safety features set up correctly.

Good to Know

Abita Springs Automatic Gate Questions, Answered

What Abita Springs owners ask before automating a gate.

6 Common QuestionsStraight answers
What Is the Difference Between Swing and Sliding Gates?
Swing gates pivot on hinges and need room to open. Sliding gates roll along the fence line and need clear space beside the opening. Your driveway layout usually points to one or the other.
Can I Add Access Control to a Pedestrian Gate in Abita Springs?
Yes. A walkway gate can use access control so only authorized people enter on foot. Pairing it with a driveway gate covers the whole property.
What Happens to an Automatic Gate During a Power Outage?
Many systems include a manual release or battery backup so the gate can still be opened. Ask about both when you choose, because an outage should never leave you locked in or out.
Are Automatic Gates Safe Around Children and Pets?
Safety sensors are designed to stop or reverse a gate that meets an obstruction, and keeping the gate area clear helps too. Have a professional set up and test the sensors on your Abita Springs gate.
Can a Business Use an Automatic Gate?
Yes. Commercial gates restrict entry to authorized people, protect assets, and give clients a professional first impression. Abita Springs businesses can choose styles and materials to match their building.
Should I Install an Automatic Gate Myself?
A professional installer is the safer route. Gate operators involve electrical work and heavy moving parts, and the safety features have to be set up correctly. Big Easy Fence installs gates for Abita Springs homes and businesses.
