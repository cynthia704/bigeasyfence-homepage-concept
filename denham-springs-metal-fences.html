01

Wrought Iron

A great choice if you want to make a unique statement. A custom wrought iron fence gives a classic yet contemporary look, and it lasts when you deal with rust or damage promptly, for example with rust-inhibiting paint each year.

Good to Know

Denham Springs Metal Fence Questions, Answered

What Denham Springs property owners ask about metal fencing.

6 Common QuestionsStraight answers
How much does a metal fence cost in Denham Springs?
The metal type, how much fence you need, labor, and accessories all affect the price. For a 4-foot-tall fence, average costs run about $8 to $12 per linear foot for aluminum, $7 to $10 for steel, $25 to $30 for wrought iron, and $10 for corrugated metal.
How long do metal fences last?
A protected hollow steel fence should last about 20 years, while solid steel, cast iron, and tubular aluminum fences can last a lifetime. We protect the finish of every metal fence to help you get the most life from it.
Which metal is best around a swimming pool?
Aluminum. It is rust-proof, strong, durable, and low maintenance, and we highly recommend it if you have a swimming pool.
Do you offer metal fence maintenance?
Yes. Our team can provide metal fence maintenance services. For wrought iron, dealing with rust or damage promptly, for example with rust-inhibiting paint each year, keeps the fence lasting.
Can you build a custom metal fence?
Yes. Our fencing professionals can make a custom metal fence that suits the design or style of your property, in iron, steel, or aluminum.
How do I get a free estimate?
Contact us any day and we will give you a free estimate for the fencing service you need, and walk you through the installation process.
