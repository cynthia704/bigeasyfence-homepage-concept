What We Fix

5 Common Fence Issues We Tackle

The real problems we see most often on New Orleans properties, and how we handle each one.

5 Common IssuesWhat we fix most often
01

Rotting Wood

Wooden fences are susceptible to rotting, especially in humid climates. We assess the extent of damage and replace affected sections.

02

Leaning or Unstable Posts

Unstable posts lead to an insecure, wobbly fence. We reinforce or replace them so your fence stands strong.

03

Broken Panels

Whether from accidents or gradual wear, we seamlessly replace broken panels, maintaining your fence's design.

04

Rust and Corrosion

Metal fences can succumb to rust. We remove it, treat the metal, and apply a fresh coat to prevent further deterioration.

05

Weather Damage

From wind damage to sun bleaching, we identify and address weather-related issues, extending your fence's lifespan.
