Materials

Wood Types We Install in Denham Springs

Softwoods from evergreen trees are the go-to for fencing.

01

Cedar

One of the most popular fence woods. Natural oil makes it insect-resistant and rot-resistant, and its tight grain and few knots keep warping low. A finish helps it last and stops graying.

02

Cypress

Also holds natural oils that resist insects and rot. It is a strong choice if you want a handsome gothic-style picket fence.

03

Redwood

A deep red color with excellent insect and rot resistance. Treating or staining it keeps it from turning gray.

04

Other Woods

Pressure-treated pine, pressure-treated fir, spruce, and white oak are also options for homes and businesses.

Replace or Repair

Signs Your Wood Fence Needs Replacing

Some damage is beyond a patch. These signs point to a replacement.

4 Warning SignsWatch for these

Rot Near the Ground

Wood that stays damp near the soil rots first. Soft or crumbling boards at the base are a serious sign.

Leaning to One Side

A fence that leans is losing its structural strength, and the posts are usually where to look first.

Split, Warped, or Missing Boards

A few bad boards can be swapped out. Many of them together point to structural problems.

Loose or Missing Fasteners

Nails and screws that have backed out or gone missing let boards shift and the whole fence loosen.

Denham Springs Wood Fence Questions, Answered

What Denham Springs property owners ask about wood.

6 Common QuestionsStraight answers
How Much Does a Wood Fence Cost in Denham Springs?
Our Denham Springs page lists an average project total of $1,711 to $3,290, or $12 to $27 per linear foot. A 6-foot-tall wood fence averages about $2,755. Materials, size, design, and labor change the final price, and a free estimate gives you your real number.
How Long Do Wood Fences Last?
Quality wood can last up to 20 years. If the fence is not made from fully treated lumber, expect closer to 10. Wood is a natural material, so some wear is expected over time.
When Should I Replace Instead of Repair a Wood Fence?
Replace when you see rot near the ground, a fence leaning to one side, many split, warped, or missing boards, or loose and missing nails and screws. A professional installer can help you judge how much life is left.
Which Wood Is Best for a Denham Springs Fence?
Cedar, cypress, and redwood are the standout choices, since each resists insects and rot. Pressure-treated pine, fir, spruce, and white oak are other options depending on budget and look.
Do You Build Wood Fences for Businesses in Denham Springs?
Yes. Big Easy Fence installs wood fences for both residential and commercial properties in Denham Springs.
How Do I Keep a Wood Fence From Turning Gray?
Cedar and redwood are commonly treated or finished to prevent graying. Ask about a finish when you choose your wood.
