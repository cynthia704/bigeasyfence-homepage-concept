Common Fixes

How We Fix the Usual Problems

Most fence damage falls into a few categories, and each one has a standard fix.

4 Common RepairsProblem and fix

Wet or Dry Rot

The affected area is cut out completely with a saw, then patched or replaced with new wood.

Rusted or Popped Nails

Old nails come out and new ones go in. It is a small job that keeps boards from working loose.

Sagging or Wobbly Fences

Metal, iron, and other fences that lean get extra support from a brace or a tension rod.

Rot That Has Spread

When rot has gone all the way through a section, replacing that section keeps it from spreading to the rest of the fence.

Repair or Replace

When Repair Makes Sense in Denham Springs

A repair usually costs less than a new fence, up to a point.

3 Key PointsWhat to expect
01

Wood Posts and Rails

A weak wood post comes out along with the adjacent panels, and a new post goes in. Steel splints or braces are often added to help the post last longer. Sagging rails are tightened, reinforced, and fastened securely.

02

Repair When Damage Is Under 20%

As a rule of thumb, if less than 20 percent of the fence is damaged, repair is the better value. The fence keeps its original look, and you avoid paying for a full rebuild.

03

Replace When Damage Passes 20%

If more than 20 percent of the panels need replacing, a new fence makes more sense. Past that point, repairs start to add up, and a new fence is usually the better investment.

Denham Springs Fence Repair Questions, Answered

What Denham Springs property owners ask before booking a repair.

6 Common QuestionsStraight answers
How Much Does Fence Repair Cost in Denham Springs?
Our Denham Springs page puts the average repair at $250 to $750, or $15 to $35 per foot. Small fixes such as holes, cracks, wood rot, or missing boards run $100 to $200, and post, panel, or leaning-fence repairs run $250 to $1,000. A free estimate breaks the price down for your fence.
Should I Repair or Replace My Fence?
A common rule of thumb is 20 percent. If less than 20 percent of the fence is damaged, repair is usually the better value. If more than 20 percent of the panels need replacing, it is time for a new fence.
What Causes Fences to Need Repair?
Fences sit outside all year, so rust, sagging, rot, and decay are ordinary wear. Moisture, mold, mildew, and insects speed up the damage, especially on wood.
What Fence Types Do You Repair in Denham Springs?
Wood, metal, wrought iron, and automatic gates, plus chain-link, vinyl, PVC, and aluminum fences. We work on residential and commercial properties and for large construction organizations.
How Are a Rotted Board and a Rotted Post Fixed?
For rot in a wood fence, the damaged area is cut out entirely and patched or replaced with new wood. A rotted post is removed along with the adjacent panels and replaced, and steel splints or braces are often added for strength. This is work best left to a repair crew.
Will You Explain the Repair Before Work Starts?
That is the plan. The estimate covers the repair process, any materials that need replacing, and a breakdown of the cost, so you know what you are paying for.
