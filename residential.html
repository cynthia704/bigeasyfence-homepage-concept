Before We Start

What We Check Before We Build

A fence that looks right but lands in the wrong spot is a headache later, not a win now — this is standard on every residential job we take on.

Property Lines

We confirm the actual boundary before digging — New Orleans lot lines are often informally surveyed.

HOA & Historic Review

Some neighborhoods require separate design approval for fence height and material.

Permits & Setbacks

We handle the local permit and setback requirements that apply to your specific address.

Utility Marking
