Good to Know

Commercial Chain Link Fence Questions, Answered

What business owners ask about chain link fencing.

7 Common QuestionsStraight answers
Is chain link a good fit for a business budget?
Yes. Chain link is an economical fencing solution that does not compromise on security, which is why it suits so many commercial properties.
What security features can be added?
We can add gate options, access control systems, and barbed wire, and we customize the setup around your specific security requirements.
Will chain link hold up to New Orleans weather?
It is built to. We use sturdy mesh with rust-resistant coatings, so the fence stands up to the New Orleans climate.
Does chain link keep my property visible?
Yes. The open design allows visibility, which helps deter potential threats while you keep an eye on your property.
How much maintenance does a chain link fence need?
Very little. Minimal upkeep makes it a practical choice for busy commercial properties.
Do your fences meet local regulations?
We design chain link fences with safety and local regulations in mind. It is still smart to confirm the requirements for your specific site during your estimate.
Can chain link be used inside a warehouse?
Yes. Chain link can create separate storage areas for valuable goods and products inside a warehouse, and it is popular in industrial, construction, and warehouse businesses.
