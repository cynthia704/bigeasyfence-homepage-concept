The Craft

What Makes Wrought Iron Different

A quick look at where wrought iron comes from and how it differs from cast iron.

2 Good to KnowHistory and materials
01

A Craft With Deep Roots

Wrought iron fencing dates back to the Colonial era, when handcrafted iron complemented stone and brick homes. Victorian-era makers added pickets, scrolls, and posts, and that ornamental look is still the style people ask for.

02

Wrought Iron vs Cast Iron

Wrought iron is heated and shaped with tools, which is why it can take so many intricate designs. Cast iron is poured into a mold and only holds that mold's shape. Authentic hand-forged wrought iron is rare and costly today, so most wrought iron style fencing is made from iron combinations. Why is wrought iron so expensive?

Good to Know

Wrought Iron Fence Questions, Answered

The real questions people ask before installing wrought iron, including cost.

7 Common QuestionsStraight answers
How Do You Care for a Wrought Iron Fence?
Rust is the main enemy. It weakens the iron and, left alone, can make the fence crumble. Rain, humidity, and scratches from nearby plants all contribute. Rust-inhibiting paint plus a protective coating of wax or water repellent helps shield the fence from moisture.
How Long Does Wrought Iron Fencing Last?
With proper care it can last a lifetime, and because it is so strong, repair costs stay low. Check regularly for rust spots and keep the fence clean, since built-up dirt and grit can damage the protective coating.
How Do You Dress Up a Wrought Iron Fence?
The patterns are decorative on their own, but you can also add elements such as curves and post toppers to give a modern wrought iron fence or gate its own style.
Can You Build a Custom Wrought Iron Design?
Yes. You can browse our wrought iron designs, or we can arrange a consultation with our fencing specialists to create an original design that fits your budget. Ornamental details cost extra, so we plan them without giving up the fence's strength and security.
How Much Does a Wrought Iron Fence Cost in New Orleans?
Our cost guide puts materials at about $15.29 per linear foot for a four-foot fence, with a range of $13.81 to $16.76. Home Advisor figures show New Orleans homeowners typically paying $1,255 to $4,176 for installation. A fence of 140 to 200 feet can run $2,660 to $5,200. Why is wrought iron so expensive?
What Makes the Price Go Up?
Corners and multiple levels, intricate barrier insert patterns, curves, post toppers, and custom work all add cost. Plain black is the least expensive finish, and green, white, bronze, and maroon are also available. A wrought iron gate can run from $300 to $9,000 in materials and installation, and heavily customized work can pass $10,000.
Does the Slope of My Yard Matter?
Yes. Metal fences install best on flat ground. If your property has several slopes or hard-to-reach areas, expect higher labor and installation costs.
