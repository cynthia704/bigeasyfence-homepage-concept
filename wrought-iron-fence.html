Caring for a Wrought Iron Fence

The real questions people ask before installing wrought iron fencing.

How do you care for a wrought iron fence?
Rust is enemy number one — it affects the strength and endurance of the iron, and left unchecked will eventually cause the fence to crumble. Rain, humidity, and scratches from nearby vegetation all contribute. Rust-inhibiting paint plus a protective coating of wax or water repellent helps protect the fence from water and moisture.
How long does wrought iron fencing last?
If properly cared for, a wrought iron fence can last a lifetime. Because it's so strong, repair costs stay low. Check regularly for rust spots, and keep the fence clean — dirt and grit left to accumulate can damage the protective coating and lead to rust.
How do you dress up a wrought iron fence?
The intricate patterns and designs are decorative in their own right, but there are also several additions available to enhance a modern wrought iron fence's unique style — post toppers, curves, and other ornamental details.
How do you maintain a wrought iron fence?
Our professional metal fabricators help you get the custom design and solution your project needs. More ornamental options — like curves and post toppers — cost more, but you should never sacrifice the fence's original function and security for style. Wrought iron is best installed on flat surfaces; sloped or hard-to-access land increases labor and installation costs.
