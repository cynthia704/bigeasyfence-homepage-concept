What Goes Wrong

Common Fence Repair Issues in Sorrento

Fences take a beating from weather, pests, and plain age.

01

Severe Weather

High winds and flooding can knock sections loose or damage them outright.

02

Sun and Moisture Wear

Constant exposure to sun and moisture wears a fence down over time.

03

Termites and Rodents

Pests damage a fence slowly, and the harm often shows up late.

04

Shifting Soil

Natural settling or changes in the climate can leave a fence uneven or unstable.

05

Accidents and Interference

Impacts and human interference can break boards, bend metal, or damage gates.

By Material

Fence Types We Commonly See Repaired

Every material fails in its own way.

2 Key PointsWhat to expect
01

Wood and Vinyl Fences

Wood is popular for its natural look and affordability, but it can rot and warp. Vinyl is durable and weather-resistant, but it can turn brittle and break over time.

02

Metal and Chain Link Fences

Metal is sturdy and long-lasting, but it can rust and corrode, so it needs regular care. Chain link is affordable and low maintenance, but strong winds and heavy objects can damage it.

Care and Upkeep

Tips for Maintaining Your Fence

Regular attention prevents the repairs you would rather not make.

01

Inspect Often

Look for cracks, broken boards, and rusted nails, and deal with them right away.

02

Clean Regularly

Remove debris, dirt, and moss, which cause damage over time.

03

Add a Protective Coating

A coating helps prevent weather damage and extends the life of the fence.

04

Trim Back Plants

Cut vegetation and trees near the fence. They cause damage and make upkeep harder.

Good to Know

Sorrento Fence Repair Questions, Answered

What Sorrento property owners ask before booking a repair.

6 Common QuestionsStraight answers
What Damages Fences in Sorrento?
Severe weather such as high winds and flooding, sun and moisture wear, termites and rodents, shifting soil, and accidents. Regular inspection helps you catch each of these early.
How Much Does Fence Repair Cost in Sorrento?
Our Sorrento page puts the average between $150 and $800. Minor jobs such as loose posts or a few pickets cost less, while replacing a whole section or fixing extensive damage costs more. The fence type, the materials, and the labor all affect the price.
Which Fence Types Can Be Repaired?
Wood, vinyl, metal, and chain link fences are all commonly repaired. Big Easy Fence handles repairs on all types of fences.
What Should I Ask a Repair Company?
Ask whether it is licensed and insured, request references, and read reviews. Get a written estimate to compare, and ask whether the work carries a warranty.
Why Is My Sorrento Fence Leaning?
Shifting soil is a common cause. Natural settling or climate changes can leave a fence uneven or unstable, and storm damage or a loose post can add to it.
How Do I Keep a Fence From Needing Repairs?
Inspect it regularly, clean off debris, dirt, and moss, and add a protective coating. Trimming plants back around a Sorrento fence also helps it last.
