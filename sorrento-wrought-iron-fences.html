Wrought Iron Versus Other Fence Types

Iron costs more up front, and here is what you get for it.

3 Key PointsWhat to expect
01

Iron Versus Wood

A wrought iron fence can last for many years with modest maintenance. Wood, by comparison, may warp or rot over time.

02

Security With Open Views

Iron is very secure and hard to breach, yet it does not block the view. That makes it a good fit for owners who want to show off a garden.

03

A Long-Term Investment

Iron is more expensive than most other fencing. It adds property value and curb appeal, so many owners in the Sorrento area treat it as a long-term investment.

Care and Upkeep

Maintaining Your Wrought Iron Fence in Sorrento

Proper care keeps an iron fence looking sharp for years.

01

Wash With Mild Soap

Clean with a mild solution of dish soap and water, then rinse with a hose. This stops dirt and grime from damaging the metal surface.

02

Inspect for Rust

Check the fence regularly for signs of rust or damage.

03

Fix Problems Early

Address any issue right away, before it turns into something more serious.

04

Add a Protective Coat

A protective coating of wax or oil can help keep rust from forming.

Good to Know

Sorrento Wrought Iron Fence Questions, Answered

What Sorrento property owners ask before choosing iron.

6 Common QuestionsStraight answers
Why Choose Wrought Iron for a Sorrento Property?
It is durable, secure, and customizable, and it looks elegant on both homes and businesses. Iron also needs less upkeep than many other fence types and can add to a property's value.
Is Wrought Iron More Expensive Than Other Fences?
Yes, it generally costs more than other types of fencing. Prices also vary a great deal between designs, so set a budget early and weigh it against the long-term value.
How Do I Care for a Wrought Iron Fence?
Wash it with dish soap and water, rinse with a hose, and inspect it for rust or damage. Fix problems right away, and use a protective coating of wax or oil to help prevent rust.
How Do I Pick a Design for My Sorrento Home?
Start with the fence's purpose, then match the design to your home's architecture and your budget. Check neighborhood guidelines or restrictions before you commit.
Why Hire a Professional to Install Iron?
Professionals bring the expertise and experience to install the fence efficiently and to required safety standards. They also have the tools and equipment to get an accurate result, which is the safer route for a Sorrento fence.
Does Iron Block the View in My Yard?
No. Iron fencing does not obstruct views, which is why it suits owners who want to show off a garden while keeping the property secure.
