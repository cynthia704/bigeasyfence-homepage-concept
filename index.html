5 Local Factors We Plan Around

Humidity Changes Everything

Standing water at a post base is one of the most common causes of early wood rot in this climate. Material and installation choices matter more here than style does.

Wood Fence Guide

Termite Pressure

Formosan termite pressure is a known factor for untreated wood fencing across the metro.

Hurricane Wind

Panel and gate hardware should be rated for storm-season gusts, not just a calm day.

Older, Informal Lot Lines

Many New Orleans properties were surveyed informally, so a line check is part of the job.

Historic-District Rules

Some neighborhoods have separate design review for fence height and material.
