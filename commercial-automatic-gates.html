Good to Know

Commercial Automatic Gate Questions, Answered

What business owners ask about automatic gates.

6 Common QuestionsStraight answers
What Access Options Are Available?
Access control and remote operation are standard options. Add-ons include intercom systems, video surveillance, and emergency access controls.
Are Safety Measures Included?
Yes. We include safety measures to protect your property and personnel, and we design gates with safety and local regulations in mind.
Will the Gate Hold Up in Our Climate?
Our gates use sturdy frames and reliable automation systems built to withstand the test of time and the New Orleans climate.
How Does a Gate Help Daily Operations?
Authorized personnel can enter and exit without manual gate operation, and you control who enters your premises and can monitor access.
Can Emergency Access Be Added?
Yes. Emergency access controls are one of the add-ons we offer, along with intercom systems and video surveillance.
How Does an Automatic Gate Work?
Sensors detect approaching vehicles or people and signal a control panel, which tells a motor to open or close the gate. Obstruction sensors help prevent accidents. Read how automatic gates work.
