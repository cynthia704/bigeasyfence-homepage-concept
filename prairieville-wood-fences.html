Wood Choices

Wood Types for Prairieville Fences

No single wood suits every yard. We weigh local weather, insect risk, and your budget before recommending one.

01

Cedar

A warm, rugged color that many clients pick first. Cedar is sensitive to moisture and can wear over time, so regular upkeep helps it last.

02

Redwood

It costs more than other woods, and it is more durable and resistant to termite damage. A low-maintenance option for a home fence.

03

Spruce

The least expensive wood fencing option and a good fit for a tight budget. Treating the wood and adding a sealant protects it and improves its look.

Styles

Post-and-Rail and Board Fence Styles

Both families come in residential and commercial versions.

2 Key PointsWhat to expect
01

Post-and-Rail Fences

An open view with real sturdiness. This style makes a rustic boundary or accent for your property, and it is an economical way to fence a perimeter. Designs include split rail, round doweled post and rail, ranch rail, and guard rail.

02

Board Fences

The pick for privacy and for hiding areas from view, since it blocks both sight and access. Designs include board-on-board, side-by-side, horizontal boards, mixed horizontal and vertical boards, and slatted wood.

What It Costs

What Sets the Price of a Wood Fence

The price moves with the project, so a quote for your property is the number that counts.

01

Type of Wood

Spruce sits at the low end, and redwood costs more. Your choice of wood is one of the biggest drivers of the total.

02

Labor

Installation labor is part of every quote. More complex builds take more time.

03

Materials

Posts, rails, boards, fasteners, and finish all count toward the cost of the job.

04

Fence Size

Longer runs mean more material and more work, so the length of your property line matters.

05

Design

A simple design costs less to build than a detailed one with more parts.

Prairieville Wood Fence Questions, Answered

What Prairieville homeowners ask about wood fencing.

6 Common QuestionsStraight answers
Which Wood Is Right for a Prairieville Fence?
There is no single best wood. We look at the local weather, how vulnerable the wood is to insects, and your budget, then help you pick from cedar, redwood, or spruce.
What Wood Fence Styles Can You Build?
Post-and-rail styles include split rail, round doweled, ranch rail, and guard rail. Board styles include board-on-board, side-by-side, horizontal, mixed, and slatted designs. Residential and commercial versions are both available.
How Much Does a Wood Fence Cost in Prairieville?
The total depends on the type of wood, labor, materials, the size of the fence, and the design. Because those change from one property to the next, a quote for your yard gives you the real number.
Can You Handle Small Projects in Prairieville?
Yes. Big Easy Fence takes on small and large wood fence projects and sees them through from start to finish.
Can Wood Be Stained or Painted?
Yes. Our consultants help you choose paint colors and stains that protect the wood against rot, decay, insects, and weathering. A finish with UV protection also helps the natural color last longer.
Do You Build Wood Fences for Businesses?
Yes. Big Easy Fence offers residential and commercial wood fence styles in Prairieville, including perimeter fences and privacy fences.
