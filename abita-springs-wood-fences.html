Styles

Wood Fence Styles for Abita Springs Yards

Four common options, from open and decorative to fully private.

01

Split Rail

Horizontal rails set between posts. It is mostly decorative and marks a boundary, so it adds little privacy or security.

02

Picket

Evenly spaced vertical boards with small gaps between them. It looks classic, and it gives more privacy than split rail without closing the yard off completely.

03

Board on Board

Vertical boards overlap in layers, which leaves few or no gaps. Expect more privacy than you get from a picket fence.

04

Wood-Look Composite

Made to look like real wood, and it resists rot and warping. Worth a look if you like the appearance and want less upkeep.

Why Wood

Why Abita Springs Homeowners Pick Wood

Four reasons wood keeps showing up in backyards.

4 Key BenefitsWhat wood delivers

Durability

Wood is a sturdy, proven material. A well-built fence holds up for many years, especially when it gets regular care.

Privacy

Solid boards block sightlines into your yard, so your family gets room to relax and your property is harder to see into from outside.

Curb Appeal

A wood fence frames the yard and can be stained or painted to suit your house. It adds to how the property looks from the street.

Variety

Different styles, heights, and finishes mean you can find a fit for your needs and your budget.

Before You Start

What to Decide Before You Build

A few choices shape the whole project.

01

Purpose

Do you want privacy, a boundary line, or a decorative edge? The answer points you toward solid boards, pickets, or split rail.

02

Height and Openness

Taller, tighter boards block more of the view. Open styles let light and air pass through.

03

Finish

Stain or paint protects wood and sets the color. Plan on repeating it over time to keep the fence looking good.

04

Upkeep

Wood is not maintenance-free. Regular checks and refinishing keep a wood fence in good shape.

Abita Springs Wood Fence Questions, Answered

Common questions from Abita Springs homeowners.

6 Common QuestionsStraight answers
What Styles of Wood Fence Are Available in Abita Springs?
Split rail, picket, and board on board are the common wood styles. Wood-look composite is an option if you want the appearance of wood with less upkeep. Each style trades off privacy, openness, and price.
How Much Does a Wood Fence Cost?
It depends on the type, style, and materials. There is no single number that fits every property, so we offer free quotes and free estimates on new projects so you can see the real cost for your yard.
Are Wood Fences Worth It in Abita Springs?
Homeowners generally find them a solid investment. A wood fence adds privacy, security, and curb appeal, and it can last many years with proper care.
Which Wood Fence Gives the Most Privacy?
Board on board, since its overlapping boards leave few gaps. Picket fences give some privacy, and split rail gives almost none because it is mostly decorative.
Is a Wood Fence Low Maintenance?
Wood is often described as low maintenance, and that holds up if you keep an eye on it. Periodic staining or painting and prompt replacement of damaged boards go a long way.
