About Madisonville

Madisonville is a small town in St. Tammany Parish, on the Tchefuncte River near its entrance to Lake Pontchartrain. It had 850 residents at the 2020 census.

A Short History of Madisonville

Jean Baptiste Baham founded the town in 1800, and it was first known as Coquille because of the shells in the area. Before the Civil War it was a port for goods moving to New Orleans, and the Lake Pontchartrain Causeway later tied it more closely to Greater New Orleans.

Things to Do in Madisonville
  • Maritime Museum Louisiana: A museum on the area's maritime history.
  • Tchefuncte River Lighthouse: A historic lighthouse that can only be reached by boat.
  • Wooden Boat Festival: A yearly gathering for boat lovers.
  • Marina del Ray: A working marina on the river.
Fun Facts About Madisonville
  • The old name "Coquille" is the French word for shell.
