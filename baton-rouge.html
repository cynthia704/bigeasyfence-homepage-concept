About Baton Rouge

Baton Rouge is the capital of Louisiana and the seat of East Baton Rouge Parish, on the east bank of the Mississippi River. It had 227,470 residents at the 2020 census.

Its name is French for "Red Stick."

A Short History of Baton Rouge

Baton Rouge was founded in 1699 and incorporated on January 16, 1817. Over the centuries it has been shaped by many nations and communities, and it grew into a major industrial and cultural center.

Things to Do in Baton Rouge
  • Louisiana State Capitol: The seat of state government.
  • Old Louisiana State Capitol: A historic landmark in downtown.
  • USS Kidd: A historic naval ship on display.
  • Magnolia Mound Plantation House: A historic house museum.
  • Baton Rouge Zoo: A family favorite.
  • Capitol Park Museum and Louisiana Art and Science Museum: Two museums for a rainy day.
Fun Facts About Baton Rouge
  • The city sits on bluffs that rise above the Mississippi River.
  • Baton Rouge is roughly 79 miles from New Orleans.
  • The Greater Baton Rouge State Fair is a yearly event.

Baton Rouge Fence Questions, Answered

What Baton Rouge property owners ask before installing a fence.

6 Common QuestionsStraight answers
Which fence types do you install in Baton Rouge?
Steel, wood, wrought iron, chain link, aluminum, and vinyl fences, for residential and commercial properties.
Which fence needs the least maintenance?
Vinyl is affordable, durable, and versatile, and it avoids the wear that rainwater causes on wood. Aluminum is durable and cost-effective, and one of our most requested options.
Can steel fencing keep the view open?
Yes. Steel fences give strong security without blocking scenic views.
Do you install gates with chain link?
Yes. We install steel or vinyl chain link fences and offer gate options to suit your needs.
Can I customize a wrought iron fence?
Yes. Wrought iron gives you plenty of room to choose your style and durability, and we build it to handle a range of weather conditions.
How long will a wood fence last?
With proper care, a well-installed wood fence lasts for years. Skilled installation matters, which is why our contractors handle the work.
