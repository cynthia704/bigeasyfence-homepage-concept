The Steel Advantage

Why Choose a Steel Fence

Strength is the headline, and there is more behind it.

6 Key BenefitsWhat steel delivers

Strength and Durability

Steel is a strong material, built to take on New Orleans heat, humidity, and the occasional storm.

Low Upkeep

A steel fence needs little care as long as its protective finish stays intact. Touch up chips promptly, since humid air can find bare metal.

Enhanced Security

Sturdy construction and real height make steel a strong deterrent, which adds peace of mind.

Custom Designs

Every property is different, and steel can be tailored to yours, from ornate designs to sleek modern styles.

Timeless Elegance

Classic, versatile designs suit a historic home or a modern one, and they fit the mix of architecture across the city.

Recyclable Material

Steel is widely recycled, which is a plus if sustainability matters to you.

Good to Know

Steel Fence Questions, Answered

What New Orleans property owners ask before choosing steel.

6 Common QuestionsStraight answers
Is a Steel Fence Secure?
Steel is sturdy, and a taller steel fence is a strong deterrent to potential intruders. Design matters too, since height and spacing both add to security. Your installer can match the fence to what you want to protect.
Does a Steel Fence Need Much Maintenance?
Steel needs little upkeep as long as its protective finish stays intact. In New Orleans humidity, touch up chips and scratches before bare metal is exposed. Aluminum is the lower-maintenance option if you want a metal fence that resists rust on its own.
What Steel Fence Styles Can I Get?
Classic designs feature scrollwork and ornate motifs, while modern designs use clean lines and minimal detail. Both can be customized to fit your property. Tell us the style of your home and we can point you toward the right direction.
Will a Steel Fence Suit an Older New Orleans Home?
Yes. Classic steel designs with scrollwork echo the character of historic homes, including those in the French Quarter. Modern designs suit newer builds. The choice comes down to your home's style and how much ornament you like.
Do You Install Steel Fences for Businesses?
Yes. Steel fences serve both residents and businesses in New Orleans. Commercial properties often choose steel for its height and sturdy construction.
How Does Steel Compare With Aluminum?
Both give an ornamental metal look. Steel is known for strength, while aluminum resists rust on its own. Our aluminum page covers that option in detail.
